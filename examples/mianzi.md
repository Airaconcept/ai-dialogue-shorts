# 面子

[远景·航拍缓推] 晨雾漫过层层茶山，一老一小两个身影在雾气里分拣茶叶 / Aerial push-in over mist-covered terraced tea mountains; an old farmer and a young man sorting tea leaves in the drifting fog

老茶农：小伙子，记住一句话——这世上最贵的东西叫面子，最不值钱的东西，也叫面子。 / Young man, remember this: the most expensive thing in this world is called face — and the cheapest, too.
年轻人：这话自相矛盾，到底是贵还是贱？ / That's self-contradictory — which is it, precious or cheap?

[特写·手部] 老茶农粗糙的手捻起一片嫩叶，对着晨光端详，叶脉透亮 / Extreme close-up of the old farmer's weathered hands holding a tender leaf up to the morning light, its veins glowing translucent

老茶农：你拼命挣面子的时候，它贵——一顿饭吃掉半个月工钱，只为换别人一句"讲究"；等人走茶凉，当初花大价钱挣来的东西，转头就一文不值。 / When you're desperate to earn it, it's dear — half a month's wages on a single dinner, just to hear someone call you generous. When the guests leave and the tea cools, what cost you dearly turns worthless overnight.

[中景·侧逆光] 年轻人若有所思，雾气从两人之间缓缓飘过 / Medium shot in soft backlight: the young man thoughtful, mist drifting slowly between the two figures

年轻人：可人活一张脸，没面子，别人看不起啊。 / But a man lives on his face — without it, people look down on you.

[近景·过肩] 老茶农把茶叶撒回竹匾，望向远山 / Over-the-shoulder close-up: the old farmer tosses the leaves back into the bamboo tray, gazing toward the distant mountains

老茶农：看不起你的人，你挣再多面子，他照样看不起；看得起你的人，你蓬头垢面，他照样敬你三分。面子从来不是挣来的，是人家心甘情愿给的。 / Those who despise you will despise you no matter how much face you earn; those who respect you will honor you even in rags. Face was never earned — it is freely given.

[近景·低角度] 老茶农端起粗瓷茶杯，吹开热气，眼神平静 / Low-angle close-up: the old farmer lifts a coarse porcelain cup, blowing the steam away, calm-eyed

年轻人：那我以后索性不要面子了？ / Then I'll simply stop wanting face at all?

[中景·环绕缓移] 雾气渐散，阳光洒在两人身上，老茶农把茶杯递给年轻人 / Slow orbiting medium shot: the mist thins, sunlight falls on both figures as the old farmer hands the tea cup to the young man

老茶农：不是不要，是分清。里子是自己的日子，面子是别人的嘴——日子过好了，嘴自然就闭上了。记住：奔着里子去的人，最后里子面子全都有；只奔着面子去的人，最后两手空空。 / Not abandon — distinguish. Substance is your own life; face is other people's mouths. Live your days well, and the mouths fall silent on their own. Remember: chase substance and you end with both; chase only face and you end with neither.
