# 争

场景：晨雾茶山

[远景] 晨雾漫过层层茶山，老茶农弯腰修剪茶树，年轻人匆匆上山 / Morning mist rolling over layered tea mountains, an old farmer pruning tea bushes, a young man hurrying up the stone path

老茶农：小伙子，记住——这世上所有赢了争吵的人，其实都输了。 / Young man, remember — everyone who wins an argument has actually lost.
年轻人：赢了怎么会输？道理明明在我这边！ / How can winning be losing? I'm clearly in the right!

[特写] 老茶农布满皱纹的手拿起修枝剪，剪下一枝老叶 / Weathered old hands snipping a tea branch with shears, an old leaf falling

老茶农：你赢了道理，输了人心。吵赢一次，疏远一分；吵赢十次，身边就没人了。 / You win the point, but lose the person. Win once, drift a little; win ten times, stand alone.
年轻人：难道被人冤枉，也一声不吭？ / So when I'm wronged, I just stay silent?

[中景] 老茶农直起身，擦了擦汗，笑了 / The old farmer straightens up, wipes sweat, smiles warmly

老茶农：分跟谁。跟讲理的人，讲道理；跟不讲理的人，讲再见。 / Depends on who. Reason with the reasonable; with the unreasonable — just say goodbye.
年轻人：那不是憋屈吗？ / Isn't that frustrating?

[近景] 老茶农解下腰间竹筒，倒了杯热茶递过去，热气升腾 / Pouring steaming hot tea from a bamboo tube into two cups, steam rising in golden light

老茶农：憋屈一时，不亏一世。记住——嘴上赢了，是输；心里赢了，才是赢。下次想吵架，先喝杯茶：茶凉得慢，气消得快。 / A win on the lips is a loss; a win in the heart is a win. Next time you want to argue, have some tea first — tea cools slowly, but anger fades fast.
